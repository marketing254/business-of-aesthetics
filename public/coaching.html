Busy every day. But is the practice actually growing?

A full schedule can hide what is quietly holding a practice back. Most owners only find out when the gaps show up months later.

  • Clients don't come back on time

    Tox clients leave without booking their next visit, and memberships go unused until they are canceled.

  • Consultations end with "I'll think about it"

    Almost every practice runs a consultation. Very few follow up with the client who left undecided.

  • Everything runs through you

    You know the numbers, you spot the problems, and you pass every fix down. You can't keep doing it all yourself.

Questions owners ask

Anything else? Email us at coaching@businessofaesthetics.org.

How is this different from coaching?

Coaching depends on a coach's time and a monthly meeting. The BOA Practice System runs on your own data. It builds your plan, tracks your 9 key numbers every month, and shows each person on your team what to fix next.

Will I work with anyone?

Yes. Your free strategy meeting is with Jeffrey Richmond, cofounder of Business of Aesthetics. After that, an implementation specialist works with your practice champion to put each system in place, while the system handles your Blueprint, monthly reports and check-ins.

What is a practice champion?

An office manager or team lead in your practice who owns the action lists, keeps the team on track between check-ins, and works with your implementation specialist. Every practice names one before starting.

Without a coach, what keeps my team on track?

Three things. The system gives each person an action list and a quick weekly check-in, and alerts you when something slips. Your practice champion keeps the team moving day to day. And your implementation specialist steps in when a system stalls.

Do you use AI?

Yes. Our Practice Analytics Technology uses AI to analyze the business numbers from your practice software against industry benchmarks, and matches what it finds to proven playbooks built from 300+ expert interviews. It builds your Blueprint from your last 3 years of data and spots problems early.

What does it cost?

The regular price is $1,497 a month. Founding members pay $897 a month, locked in for as long as they stay. It is a flat fee, with no revenue share. The commitment is 12 months.

How much time does it take?

Reading your monthly report takes a few minutes. Each weekly check-in takes your team under a minute. Your practice champion needs a little time each week to keep the action lists moving.

What do you need from my practice?

Access to your practice software, or a short monthly export, a practice champion, and the team members who own each number.

How do you handle access to our data?

We only pull the business numbers needed for your Blueprint and reports, outside practice hours. We walk you through exactly how access works in the strategy meeting, before you sign anything.

Which practice software do you work with?

We confirm yours in the strategy meeting. Some systems let us pull the numbers directly; with others you send a short monthly export.

What if it doesn't work for us?

Your Blueprint sets goals from your own numbers, and every quarter it is updated against your results. If a number isn't moving, we change the plan instead of waiting. And because we check your data and fit before you sign, you know what you're committing to from the start.

Do I need to be an Ekwa Marketing client?

No. The BOA Practice System stands on its own.
